Using Poly-Encoders for Computationally Efficient Automated Creativity Assessment

With Philip Gregory and Jiho Noh, I developed a BERT-based Poly-Encoder to score creativity in scientific responses. On about 18,000 human-rated responses from the Scientific Creative Thinking Test, the DeBERTa-v3-Large model reached a Pearson correlation of 0.74 with human ratings, while scoring each response in 0.010 seconds on a consumer-grade Intel i5 CPU.

  • Evaluated encoder families and poly-code counts to study the trade-off between model performance and efficiency.
  • Explored a locally deployable alternative to large language models for classroom assessment; the study also discusses limits in generalizing to unseen prompts and potential scoring bias.

Learning Heuristics for Constraint Programming

As a self-guided research project, I explored whether neural networks could learn useful heuristics for constraint programming puzzle solvers. I trained CNN and GNN models to guide solver decisions and iteratively refined their predictions.

  • Reached 94% post-training prediction accuracy.
  • Built the models with PyTorch Lightning and tracked experiments and metrics with Weights & Biases.
